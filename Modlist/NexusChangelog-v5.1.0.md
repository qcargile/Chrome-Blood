## 🛠 Key Notes

- **A Small 2.5 Update**\
  Mostly catching mods still being updated for 2.5, dropping troublesome content, and picking up a few quality-of-life fixes. No new game needed if you're coming from 5.0.
- **Combat Tweaks**\
  A light retune to health and damage values. Current health multipliers are 0.35 for ordinary enemies, 0.40 for elites, MaxTac and machines, and 0.50 for bosses. Enemy blade and blunt damage multipliers are 1.20. Damage Scaling and Balance - Extended 4.1 also adds a separate dual-wield damage setting.
- **Much Better AI 2.4**\
  Adjustments to enemy repositioning, nearby combat coordination, and tracking moving targets. Movement still helps you avoid incoming fire.
- **Small Fixes and Additions**\
  Audio, inventory, save-loading, dual-wielding and shotgun-shell fixes, alongside Kinetic Gorilla Arms and Savage Stun. Plan C returns, and ReImagined is enabled again.
- **Setup and MO2 Tools**\
  [Setup 1.1.0](https://github.com/qcargile/CyberpunkModlistSetup/releases/tag/v1.1.0) adds clearer cleanup options, file-preservation choices, changeable folders and an obvious way to skip preparation. The included MO2 update notice also catches changed Wabbajack packages when the list's version number stays the same.
- **Keep Reporting Problems**\
  Patch 2.5 was rough on a lot of mods. It may take a while for authors to get things stable and crash-free again. Keep reporting bugs and crashes, and I'll do my best to help.
