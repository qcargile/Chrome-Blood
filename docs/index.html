00 · BRIEFING

What is Chrome & Blood

Chrome & Blood changes how you fight, build your character, earn money, and get around Night City.

Chrome & Blood 5.1.0 uses one Standard profile, balanced around Very Hard. The Player Guide explains the details in-game.

01 · COMBAT

Combat

Enemy health is lower, while armor, abilities, and equipment give tougher enemies more protection.

  • Much Better AI and Cyber Enemies make squads coordinate, reposition, and deploy new cyberware abilities
  • ME Armor adds visible armor segments that reward penetration, target selection, and sustained pressure
  • Faction traits and reinforcements make encounters distinct and unpredictable
  • Damage Scaling and Balance adjusts weapon damage and enemy health
02 · BUILDS

Builds

New perks and cyberware expand your build options, with revised slot limits and capacity costs.

  • 18 new perks via Merc Protocol — designed specifically for this list
  • Expanded cyberware through Neuralware and the Cyberware Improved series
  • Realistic slot limits and cyberware capacity costs
03 · STEALTH & HACKING

Stealth & Hacking

Enemies react to noise, bodies, and suspicious activity. Netrunning has new progression, breach, and trace mechanics.

  • Enemies investigate noise, react to bodies and takedowns, and search your last known position
  • Enemy netrunners reposition and support their squad instead of standing still
  • Much Better Netrunning overhauls hacking from the ground up
04 · VISUALS

Visuals

Updated lighting, weather, materials, and textures change the look of the city.

  • Nova City 2 transforms the visual foundation
  • Nova Optics, LUT Switcher, and tuned weather give you control without losing the list's visual direction
  • Upgraded fog, LOD, vegetation, path tracing, and civilian variety improve the city at street level and in the distance
05 · VEHICLES

Vehicles

Reworked handling and more cars and bikes to buy.

  • Added cars and bikes through Virtual Car Dealer
  • Handling reworked by Surface Type Expansion and All vehicles can steer
06 · THE WORLD

The World

Repeatable fixer jobs, new encounters, and changes to shops and services give you more to do between story missions.

  • Much Better Contracts — repeatable fixer assignments, linked Operations, and crafting-spec rewards through The Wire on the Net
  • Immersive Fixers — changes how fixer work becomes available
  • Reworked ammunition changes how much you can carry
  • Economy rebalanced through Much Better Eddies — steeper scaling, bigger payouts, and phone-ordered services
07 · STANDARD PROFILE

Playing Chrome & Blood 5.1.0

Standard is the only player profile in 5.1.0. It includes the full combat, AI, netrunning, economy, progression, and vehicle overhaul.

  • Limited HUD is enabled by default; no special implant is required for the HUD.
  • Reload manually and watch your ammunition. Reloading early usually discards the rounds left in the magazine.
  • Fast travel charges a fare and advances the clock. Dialogue checks are hidden.
  • Dark Future Core is included as an optional module and disabled by default.
  • Some graphical mods are included but temporarily disabled while we wait for their Cyberpunk 2.5 updates.
  • Buy Chrome Ballistics weapons, attachments, and schematics from physical gunsmiths and melee vendors.

Updating to 5.1.0: save compatible with v5.0. No new game needed. Back up your saves and personal settings before updating, then select Chrome & Blood - Standard Profile in MO2.

Read Rule 11 before adding your own mods, and use the [NoDelete] tag to preserve additions across updates.
